Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the Tiny Universe website and Tiny Universe Publisher, a private tool the Tiny Universe team uses to publish videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account and to read the statistics of those accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Only the Tiny Universe team's own YouTube channel and Instagram account, after the team signs in and grants access.
  • It uploads our own videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and release times, and reads upload status and analytics for our own channel and account (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, audience countries and age/gender groups as aggregated by the platforms).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or any other users. The app is not offered to the public.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Aggregated statistics are kept privately by the team to improve our videos.

Google and YouTube

Tiny Universe Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Meta and Instagram

Instagram publishing and statistics use the Meta Graph API under Meta's Platform Terms. Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
